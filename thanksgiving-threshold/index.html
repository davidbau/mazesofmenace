Thanksgiving Threshold

November 26, 2026

Happy Thanksgiving, and congratulations to everyone who’s taken part in the Mazes of Menace challenge! Several entries now match 100% of screens, and more than ten are above 97%. Getting NetHack to agree with you for that many screens is quite a feat.

At least ten entries will qualify for Phase 2. Phase 1 ends and Phase 2 begins at 00:00 UTC on Sunday, November 29, 2026. In Eastern time, that’s Saturday, November 28, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. EST.

Preparing Your Code for Phase 2

At the end of Phase 1 on Saturday, we will take a fixed snapshot of your code that will serve as your permanent, immutable baseline for Phase 2. Make sure your Phase 1 code is as well-structured and as maintainable as possible before that checkpoint.

In Phase 2, we will score maintainability based on changes to your JavaScript—specifically, the JavaScript that actually forms the runtime of your engine. It is fine if that JavaScript is the output of a compiler or transpiler, so long as the runtime JavaScript itself is clean and maintainable; we will be examining diffs of your runtime JavaScript, and we won’t be scoring diffs of TypeScript, C, Lua, or other precursor files.

Phase 2 scoring will be linked to the size of traditional Unix diffs between your new code and your Phase 1 submission. In particular, avoid jamming together very long lines in your JavaScript source; for example, a single 400 KB line of code is not very maintainable and will likely damage your Phase 2 score. If you cannot cleanly diff your Phase 2 code against your final Phase 1 checkpoint on Saturday, your Phase 2 submission may receive a low score.

Enjoy the holiday, and good luck on your final Phase 1 runs!
